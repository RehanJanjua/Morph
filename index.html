Supported formats
Not supported yet

Audio and video are limited to the outputs listed. Not available as outputs: amr, ape, wv, alac, dts, caf, 3gp, 3g2, f4v, mxf, dv, vob, asf, mts and m2ts. MIDI, DSF and DFF cannot be read. Doc, wpd, wps, pages, xps, djvu, mobi, azw3, fb2, cbr, presentations (pptx, ppt, odp and variants) and fonts need a server-side engine such as LibreOffice or dedicated libraries. Images: jp2, jxl, tga, dds, exr, hdr, psd, xcf, ai, eps, ps, pcx, pbm, pgm, wbmp, icns and camera RAW (dng, cr2, cr3, nef, arw, orf, raf). Archives: 7z, rar, bz2, xz, zst, cab, iso, wim, arj, lzh. All 3D and CAD, scientific, geospatial, email, disk-image and font formats, plus toml, ini, sql and similar developer files. Files with these extensions can still be compressed to ZIP or GZ.

Metadata such as EXIF, colour profiles and tags is not carried over. ZIP and TAR keep file timestamps. Originals are never modified; every result is a new file.
